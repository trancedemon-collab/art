TV Show Tracker

Plain-text episode tracker
Show names are inferred from filenames. Episodes are sorted by season/episode numbers when present (S01E01, 1x01, Episode 01, etc.). Watched status is saved in your browser. “Open” plays the selected local file in a lightweight built-in Firefox player. Picture adjustments are saved per episode. Browsers do not expose the real local path or reliably allow a webpage to launch VLC/another external player.
Lightweight mode: picture adjustments use CSS filters where possible. Gamma is approximated to avoid expensive frame-by-frame processing.
